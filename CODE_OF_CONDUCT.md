# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and welcoming
- Using inclusive language
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Helping newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints
- Sharing knowledge openly

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment
- Trolling
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information
- Offensive comments
- Spam

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub
- Discussions
- Issues
- Pull Requests
- Documentation
- Community chat

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainer.
